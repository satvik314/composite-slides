What keeps us up at night

  1. Concentration. Top 10 customers are 34% of ARR. Mitigation: enterprise expansion plus self-serve motion narrows this to ~28% by Q4.
  2. Infra single-vendor risk. Migration created one deep dependency. Mitigation: portability layer scoped for Q3, 3-week effort.
  3. Competitor pricing pressure. Two funded entrants discounting 40%+. We hold price; win rate is steady — we watch churn cohorts monthly.
The askApprove the Q3 hiring plan (+18 roles, 70% GTM) and the $1.2M infra portability budget. Both are inside the existing envelope — no change to runway.
